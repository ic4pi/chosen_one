# Kundala — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026_

## The short version

Kundala does not collect anything, does not send anything anywhere, and has no servers.

## The longer version

**What Kundala stores.** Your Sun sign, your birthdate if you entered one, your zodiac and
level preferences, your notification settings, which add-on packs you have unlocked, any
coupon codes you have redeemed, and your journal entries.

**Where it stores it.** In your device's local browser storage, inside the app. Nowhere else.

**What Kundala sends.** Nothing. The app contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no
advertising, no tracking pixels, no third-party SDKs, and no network requests of any kind.
The astronomical calculations that produce your fasting window run on your device rather
than on a server, which is why the app works with the network switched off.

**Notifications.** Every notification is generated and scheduled on your device by your
operating system. There is no push service, no notification server and no device token. The
content of your notifications is never transmitted.

**Purchases.** If you buy an add-on, the transaction is handled entirely by Google Play under
Google's own privacy policy. Kundala never sees your payment details and receives no personal
information from the purchase.

**Coupon codes.** Codes are verified mathematically inside the app. Redeeming one contacts
nothing and tells no one.

**Third parties.** There are none.

**Children.** Kundala is not intended for anyone under 18, and the setup screen says so.
Extended fasting is not appropriate for children or adolescents.

**Your data, deleted.** Settings → *Erase everything on this device* removes your profile,
unlocks and journal permanently. Uninstalling the app does the same. Since nothing was ever
sent anywhere, there is nothing else to delete and no one to ask.

**Changes.** If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published here and the
date above will change.

## Health notice

Kundala is a calendar and an educational guide. It is not medical advice, not a medical
device, and not a substitute for a qualified clinician. Extended fasting carries real risks.
Do not fast if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, have type 1 diabetes or take insulin, have
a current or past eating disorder, are underweight, or are under 18. Consult your doctor
before fasting if you take any medication or have any chronic condition. Break the fast
immediately if you faint, feel your heart racing or irregular, become confused, cannot keep
water down, or feel frightened by any symptom.
