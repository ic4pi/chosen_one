## Full description (4000)

```
Once every lunar month, the Moon crosses the sign your Sun was in when you were born. It stays a little over two days.

Kundala brackets that passage — one day before it arrives, one day after it leaves — and walks you through the four days in between.

YOUR WINDOW, NOT A CALENDAR

Kundala calculates the real positions of the Sun and Moon on your device. Your window is keyed to your own Sun sign, so it falls on different dates than anyone else's, and it drifts about two days earlier each month — because the sidereal month is 27.3 days and the calendar is not.

Enter your birthdate and Kundala places your Sun exactly, including the cusp cases where being born on the 22nd puts you in a different sign than the magazine says. Or just tap your sign.

Both zodiacs are supported: tropical, measured from the equinox, and sidereal with the Lahiri ayanamsa, as used in Jyotisha.

FOUR PHASES

01 TAPER — the day before. Close the kitchen, load minerals, wind digestion down.
02 THRESHOLD — the Moon enters your Sun. Hunger arrives in waves; each one passes.
03 DEPTH — the clear hours. Hunger usually drops away and something wider takes its place.
04 RETURN — the day after. How you leave a fast decides what it was worth.

Three intensity levels: Sattvic Light, Liquid, and Water Only. Start gentle. The window comes back every month.

SAFETY FIRST, GENUINELY

Kundala screens for the hard contraindications before you begin, names the symptoms that mean break the fast now, and puts as much care into refeeding as into fasting — because that is the part that can actually hurt you. Breaking early is never treated as a failure.

Kundala is not medical advice and not a clinician. It does not know your bloodwork.

WORKS COMPLETELY OFFLINE

No account. No sign-up. No analytics, no ads, no trackers, no servers. Kundala makes no network requests of any kind — the astronomy runs on your phone. Everything you enter stays on your device.

ADD-ONS, ONE-TIME

THE KEEPER — Kundala reaches out to you. A preparation countdown from up to fourteen days out, each notification carrying the one thing worth doing at that distance. Live alerts the moment the Moon crosses into and out of your Sun. Morning and evening practice prompts during the fast. And the refeeding alert at the close. Every notification is generated on your device.

THE SERPENT PATH — free, unlocked with a code from the TikTok channel. Pranayama, kriyas, bandhas and asana from the hatha and tantric traditions, matched to the phase you are in, taught step by step with their contraindications. Plus five plain-language study pieces on the nadis, the centres, and why the Moon in your Sun. It adds to the free version or to The Keeper — it changes no prices and locks nothing that was open.

No subscriptions. Nothing you already have is ever taken away or moved behind a new price.

---

Kundala is a calendar and an educational guide, not a medical device. Extended fasting carries real risks. Do not fast if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, have diabetes, have a current or past eating disorder, are underweight, or are under 18. Consult your doctor before fasting if you take any medication or have any chronic condition.
```
